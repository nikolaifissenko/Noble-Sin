# NOBLE SIN — SINGLE COVER ART BRIEF
## "Nebo" (небо)

> For the designer, AI image tool, or art director executing this cover.  
> Nebo = "sky" in Ukrainian. The single is a call for peace — soldiers disobeying orders.

---

## THE CONCEPT

The track is raw and aggressive, but the message is refusal. Not violence — the moment before or after it, when someone puts the gun down. The cover should hold that tension: power without triumph, stillness that feels earned not peaceful.

**Sky as motif.** Nebo means sky. The sky is the one thing that belongs to no army, no border, no flag. It's neutral territory above the war. That's the visual anchor.

---

## ART DIRECTION

### Composition
A figure — or the suggestion of a figure — against sky. The figure should feel like weight against vastness. Not heroic framing (no heroic low angle looking up). Either:

**Option A — Figure from above**  
Shot from high, looking down at a single figure on concrete or rubble. Sky is gone; the figure is alone in a grey space. The "sky" is the title, the absence. Stark and graphic.

**Option B — Figure facing sky, back to camera**  
A person (in heavy clothing — not military costume, but weight implied) standing, back to us, looking up at a sky that takes up 2/3 of the frame. Sky is bruised — dark clouds but with one break of light. Not redemptive light. Just light.

**Option C — Pure typography / graphic**  
No figure at all. Large treatment of NEBO in the primary typeface against a sky photograph or texture — desaturated, almost monochrome, high contrast. Type is massive. The band name is small below. The sky is texture, not scenery.

---

### Visual Treatment
- **Color:** Near-monochrome. Dark sky tones with strong contrast. No color except possibly a single muted detail — the break in the clouds, or a fragment of something red (not a flag, not explicit).
- **Grain:** Heavy photographic grain or texture. This is not clean.
- **Format:** Square (3000×3000px minimum for streaming). Also prepare 4:5 for Instagram.
- **Bleed:** Band name and single title must have clear space from edges — min 5% margin.

---

### Typography on Cover
```
NEBO

NOBLE SIN
```

NEBO: Primary typeface, all caps, massive — takes up 1/3 of the canvas width minimum.  
NOBLE SIN: Same typeface, significantly smaller, bottom-right or bottom-center.  
No subtitle, no "feat.", no release date on the cover image itself.

---

### What This Cover Is NOT
- A Ukrainian flag or any flag
- A soldier in uniform (too literal, too illustrative)
- A dove (too literal)
- An explosion or battlefield scene (wrong tone — this is refusal, not spectacle)
- Cinematic / filmic with color grading — keep it graphic and flat
