## COVER ART CONCEPT

**Direction:** One figure, not the full band, low angle from the ground looking up. Camo pants planted at the bottom of the frame, both arms raised overhead holding a white shirt open against the sky — mid-motion, like a surrender flag catching wind. Overcast "nebo" sky fills the top two-thirds. Desaturated, near-monochrome grade, slight grain — documentary, not glossy rock-poster gloss.

**Why one figure, not five:** the song's point is "any soldier, any army" — one anonymous body reads as universal. Five faces in camo reads as "the band in costume" and undercuts the message. It's also a stronger thumbnail at Spotify's 60px cover size.

**Critical constraint — stay symbol-neutral:** the song references four live, active conflicts in one line. The visual must not pick a side or read as a specific army's uniform:
- No national flags
- No unit patches or insignia
- No camo pattern tied to a specific country's military (generic olive/woodland only)
- No recognizable landmarks (keep locations "anywhere urban")

**Typography:** condensed bold sans (Anton / Bebas Neue / Archivo Black). "NOBLE SIN" small at top, "NEBO" large at bottom. Minimal, no extra graphics.

**Export spec:** 3000×3000px, PNG, sRGB (Spotify/DistroKid requirement).

Full illustrated composition reference (crop guide, shot list, wardrobe/lighting notes) published as a Claude artifact: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8395f15a-0896-4a23-a9d1-37581728ee26

## PHOTOSHOOT SHOT LIST (13/09/2026)

Wardrobe: plain olive/woodland camo pants (no patches), plain white shirt (no logos/prints).
Light: overcast or golden hour, flat/diffuse, avoid harsh midday shadows.

1. **The cover shot** — low angle, camo legs bottom third, white shirt raised overhead against the sky. Shoot per member individually, pick the strongest frame after.
2. **Group line-up** — all five shoulder to shoulder against a flat urban wall (concrete/roller shutter/chain-link, no recognizable Rome landmarks), white shirts, arms slightly raised, no smiling.
3. **Individual portraits** — each member alone, tight crop, looking up and off-frame toward the sky.
4. **Detail/texture** — hands raising a white shirt, close crop, no faces. For teaser content, not the cover.
5. **Staggered depth group shot** — backs to camera, facing the sky. Alina and two members close to camera (foreground), remaining two members further down the street (background), creating depth. White shirts, urban ground, sky dominant at top.
