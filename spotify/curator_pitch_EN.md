## NOTES

- Target: 20–30 curators per single release cycle
- Playlists to target: alt-rock, indie rock, Italian rock, female-fronted rock, new rock finds
- Platforms to use: SubmitHub (paid credits, higher response rate), Groover (paid), direct DM (free, slower)
- Send pitch minimum 3 weeks before release date (4 weeks ideal)
- Track responses in the pipeline tracker
- Do NOT send the same pitch to the same curator twice in a row — vary the angle
