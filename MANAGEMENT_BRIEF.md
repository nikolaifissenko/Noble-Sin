## 8. ONE-SHEET / EPK REQUIREMENTS

A booking one-sheet should contain:
- Band name + genre + city
- Short bio (3–4 lines max — punchy, no clichés)
- Key stats: Spotify monthly listeners, Instagram followers, notable shows
- 1 press photo (full band, high quality)
- Featured tracks (2–3 Spotify links)
- Live video link (YouTube or similar)
- Contact info (booking manager email/phone)
- Social handles

Format: 1-page PDF, clean layout, no clutter.  
Language: Italian version + English version (for international festivals).
