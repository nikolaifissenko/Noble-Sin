## Short bio (about 50 words)
Noble Sin is a five piece alternative rock band from Rome with a Ukrainian singer, a Russian guitarist and three Italians. Their new single "Nebo" ("sky" in Ukrainian), written by singer Alina in 2022, follows three sergeants in three armies who refuse to fire, and ends in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

## Long bio (about 150 words)
Noble Sin is a five piece alternative rock band based in Rome. Singer Alina Lykholat is Ukrainian and has lived in Rome since 2011. Guitarist and manager Nikolai Fissenko is Russian and grew up in Paris. Bassist Valerio, Antonio and Andrea are Italian. Nikolai and Alina met by chance in a restaurant, when a friend told them to make music together.

Their debut album "Off We Go" came out first. In October 2026 they released "Nebo", Ukrainian for "sky". Alina wrote the lyrics in 2022: three sergeants in three different armies stop obeying orders, because there are more soldiers than officers. "We are an ocean against a drop of evil." The sergeants are invented, but the voices in the song are real, voice notes sent by friends living in those countries. The final refusal comes in English, Ukrainian and Russian at once.

On stage the band is direct, loud and guitar driven, in the line of Idles, Savages and Fontaines D.C.

## Why FOCUS Wales? (if asked)
FOCUS Wales brings bands from every country to one small city for three days, which is what our band is on a small scale: a Ukrainian and a Russian playing in the same band, with three Italians. We would love to bring "Nebo" to Wrexham and meet the people who make that exchange happen.
