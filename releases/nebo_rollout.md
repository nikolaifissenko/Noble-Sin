## CONCEPT SUMMARY

Nebo is not an anti-war song in the obvious sense. It is a song about refusal — soldiers from different sides (an English-speaking sergeant, Sergeant Al-Hassan, Sergeant Grishchenko) each independently refusing to fire. The final moment delivers the same refusal in English, Ukrainian, and Russian simultaneously.

The title — sky — is never explained in the lyrics. It sits above the song as the one territory no army has claimed.

**Press angle:** "A rock song where the soldiers disobey" — simple, specific, not preachy.

---

## VISUAL CONCEPT

**White clothing throughout.** White = surrender, peace, the opposite of a uniform. Every piece of visual content connected to this single features the band or subjects dressed in white.

**Cover art:** Four-quadrant editorial image (Vogue-style photography) — one woman per quadrant, each representing a different people affected by conflict:
- Ukrainian woman / Kremlin, Moscow
- Russian woman / bombed ruins, Kyiv
- Palestinian woman in white hijab / destroyed street, Gaza
- Israeli woman in white tichel / Western Wall, Jerusalem

**Title treatment on cover:**
```
НЕБО • سماء • שָׁמַיִם • НЕБО
```
Written in four languages — Ukrainian, Arabic, Hebrew, Russian. One word, four scripts, no translation needed.

**Band reels:** All five members filmed in white clothing. Same rule as the cover — white is the visual signature of this single across every format.
