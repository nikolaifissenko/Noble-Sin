# NOBLE SIN — VISUAL IDENTITY GUIDE

> Reference document for designers, photographers, social media, and release artwork.  
> Last updated: June 2026

---

## 1. IDENTITY IN ONE SENTENCE

Noble Sin is an alternative rock band from Rome with female vocals — raw, specific, and European. The visual identity reflects that: high contrast, no decoration, no warmth that wasn't earned.

---

## 2. CORE AESTHETIC

**Direction:** Post-punk / art-rock graphic language. Bold and stark, not soft.  
**Feel:** A Roman street at 3am. Grain. Concrete. Something about to happen or already over.  
**NOT:** Smooth gradients, neon, motivational typography, lens flares, vintage sepia nostalgia.

---

## 3. COLOR PALETTE

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Primary background | Black | `#0A0A0A` | Covers, headers, dark frames |
| Primary foreground | Off-white | `#E8E4DC` | Text, graphic elements |
| Accent | Deep red | `#8B0000` | Sparingly — one element per composition max |
| Secondary neutral | Cool grey | `#3A3A3A` | Mid-tones, borders |

**Rule:** No more than 3 colors in any single composition. High contrast is the goal — if you need to add a color to make something readable, increase contrast instead.

---

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY

**Primary typeface:** Something geometric and industrial — Grotesk category.  
Suggestions (all free/licensed):
- **Neue Haas Grotesk** — first choice if budget allows
- **Inter** — free, clean, works at all sizes
- **Space Grotesk** — free, slightly harder character, good for covers

**Rules:**
- All caps for band name and single titles on covers
- No script fonts, no serif fonts except sparingly in body copy for contrast
- Type should be large and direct — if it looks "designed," scale it up until it looks inevitable
- Tracking: tight to normal. Never loose/airy.

---

## 5. PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

**Mood:** Candid and unposed, or deliberately stark. Never in-between (no "relaxed" group shots).  
**Lighting:** Hard light, single source. Side-lit or back-lit. Avoid flat studio lighting.  
**Color treatment:** Desaturate to near-monochrome or push contrast until detail compresses. Deep shadows are fine.  
**Framing:** Close crops, or full-distance with architectural context (Rome — use it). Not mid-range "band standing in a park."  
**Background:** Urban. Concrete, stone, metal, graffiti. Avoid nature unless it's harsh (cliffs, dead fields).  
**Expression:** Neutral to intense. No smiling at the camera.

---

## 6. LOGO / BAND NAME USAGE

There is no finalized logo yet. Until one is commissioned, the band name is set as:

```
NOBLE SIN
```

In whatever primary typeface is in use, all caps, no special characters, no tagline underneath.  
On dark backgrounds: off-white.  
On light backgrounds: black.  
Never outline, shadow, or bevel the text.

---

## 7. SOCIAL MEDIA TEMPLATES

**Instagram grid logic:** Alternate between two content types visually — performance (dark, contrasty) and graphic/text (stark, typographic). No pastel story templates, no colourful quote cards.  
**Story frames:** Black background, white text. No gradients.  
**Thumbnail style for Reels/TikTok:** One strong frame with the band name or track title in the corner. Same type treatment as above.

---

## 8. THINGS THAT BREAK THE IDENTITY

- Any design that looks like it belongs to a pop or EDM act
- Emoji in official artwork
- Busy collages with 5+ elements
- Filters that add warmth (golden hour, vintage grain + sepia together)
- Stock photo backgrounds
- Anything referencing the Colosseum, pasta, or Italian tourism clichés

---

## 9. REFERENCE TOUCHSTONES (aesthetic mood, not direct imitation)

- Idles — raw, political, typographically direct
- Fontaines D.C. — European street-level grittiness
- Savages — stark black/white, no apology
- Arca — willingness to be uncomfortable visually
- Joy Division / Factory Records era design — grid, contrast, function over decoration
