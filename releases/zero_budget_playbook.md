# Zero-Budget Single Rollout Playbook (2026)
> Researched 24/09/2026. This covers how to release a single with €0 spend beyond the DistroKid Musician Plus subscription the band already pays. The Nebo-specific actions are in §7 and are also written into `releases/nebo_rollout.md`. Sources are at the bottom.

---

## 1. The core idea: with no money, the lever is listener quality, not reach

Spotify's recommendation systems (Release Radar, Discover Weekly, Radio, autoplay) mostly react to **how** people listen, not **how many** people listen:
- **Release Radar** goes to followers, and it's the only guaranteed placement. It's automatic if the track is pitched in Spotify for Artists at least 7 days before release. So **follower count is a direct lever.**
- **Discover Weekly, Radio and autoplay** respond to behaviour: saves, repeat listens, playlist adds, and low skip rates. Third-party analyses (Chartlex, Playlist Supply) put the thresholds that trigger algorithmic pickup at roughly **>20% save rate** and **>2 streams per listener**, usually within 10–14 days. These are industry estimates, not Spotify figures. The direction is solid, but don't treat the numbers as exact.
- The practical consequence: 300 people who save the song and replay it are worth more than 3,000 who stream it once from a random playlist. **Every action below is built to reach the right few hundred people, not the most people.**

## 3. The zero-budget system, in order of impact

### 3.1 One song is a content library. Batch it before release.
Aim for **20–30 short vertical pieces** cut from one track, produced in 1–2 sessions **before** T-3 weeks so posting never depends on inspiration. The raw material already exists (the 14/09 shoot). Formats that work for bands:
- Hook-first performance cut: the chorus or strongest riff lands in the **first 2 seconds**.
- Story-to-camera: why the song exists (for Noble Sin, the §0 angle: Alina and Nikolai together).
- Lyric-on-screen of the single best line ("We are an ocean against a drop of evil").
- Studio/process: how the riff was built, playthrough of a part.
- Reaction/duet bait: an open question or a stitchable moment.

Rules: 3–5 Reels/TikToks per week around release; post **native** uploads with no TikTok watermark on IG (each platform downranks the other's watermark); DM shares and saves count far more than likes, so the caption should give a reason to send it to someone.

### 3.2 Test before you commit (Trial Reels)
Post 3–4 hook variants as **Instagram Trial Reels** at T-3/T-2 weeks. After ~24h, keep the one with the best watch-through and shares as the release-day Reel and the TikTok lead. It costs no money and no follower fatigue.

### 3.3 Build the follower base before release day (feeds Release Radar)
Every pre-release post points to the **HyperFollow link**, and a pre-save also follows the artist on Spotify, which grows the Release Radar audience. Pinned post, bio link, stories with link sticker, and ask each band member to push it to their own contacts personally (one-to-one messages beat broadcast posts).

### 3.4 Pitch humans directly (curators, press, radio)
The free channel that actually works is personal, specific outreach, ~20–30 targets per single:
- **Curators:** find alt-rock / post-punk / anti-war / protest-song playlists whose followers are real (engagement, not just follower count). Check who curates them, then send a short, personal email or DM. Use SubmitHub free credits and PlaylistPal's daily free pitches on top.
- **Press:** Italian webzines (Rockit, Rockambula, ImpattoSonoro, Indie-Roccia, All Music Italia's release listings) with an embargoed private link. The story (Ukrainian + Russian in one band, an anti-war song ending in three languages) is what makes an unpaid writer bother.
- **Radio:** Radio Indieffusione (submit through its artist community), Rome and regional community/web radio.
- Never send an attachment first: a private streaming link plus 5 lines and one photo.

### 3.5 Concentrate the first 48 hours
The first 2 days set the save and repeat signals. Ask fans for one specific action ("save it and add it to a playlist of yours today"), not a generic "go listen". Band members message their own networks on release morning. Only post "out now" after confirming it's live.

### 3.6 Keep the single alive after week 1
- Keep posting clips of it for 4+ weeks (the plan already says this).
- **Waterfall option (free, needs Musician Plus custom ISRC):** release the next single as a 2-track release that includes Nebo with **its original ISRC**. Nebo's streams and playlist adds carry over, and it gets a second Release Radar push. Decide this when planning Single 2.
- **Spotify's 1,000-stream rule:** a track needs ≥1,000 streams in 12 months (with a minimum of unique listeners) to earn recording royalties at all. It's not a promo metric, but it's the first real bar for each single.

## 5. What to stop doing / avoid
- Paying for Groover when the budget is zero (it was in the Nebo plan).
- Planning a Spotify Countdown Page for a single (not eligible).
- Planning Spotify Clips (discontinued).
- Chasing follower-count playlists. A 50k-follower playlist of passive listeners drags the save rate down and can look like artificial streaming.
- Posting "out now" as the only release content. The weeks after release are where the algorithm decides.

## 6. Blind spots to decide on (Nikolai)
1. **Personal risk of the §0 angle.** An openly anti-war song fronted by a Russian guitarist, pushed hard to press, can have legal consequences under Russia's "discrediting the army" laws for anyone who travels there or has family there. It's worth a deliberate decision on how prominently Nikolai's name and face lead, before the press push on 09/10, not after.
2. **The pitch collision is real but already solved by timing.** Spotify allows one unreleased song pitched at a time, so Single 2 (20/11) can only be pitched **once Nebo is live on 23/10**. That's exactly 4 weeks out, so there's no slack: submit Single 2 on DistroKid before 23/10 and pitch it on the morning of 23/10.
3. **The monthly-listener number is the unlock.** At 3,000+ monthly listeners, Countdown Pages become available for a future EP/album. Worth tracking as a milestone.
