# Noble Sin: story bank (for pitches, press releases and interviews)
> Source: Nikolai, 08/10/2026. Nikolai said: "stick to what I say". Use only these facts and don't embellish them. Every outreach email draws its personal details from here (see `spotify/outreach_rules.md`).

## How the band started
- Nikolai and Alina met by chance **at a restaurant**. An American friend said: "Nikolai plays guitar, Alina sings, do something." That's how the band started.
- **Valerio** (bass) is 100% Roman. He's also the band's events manager and personal trainer. He and Nikolai met through a dating-style app for musicians, so the band jokes that they "met on Tinder".
- **Antonio** was found by Valerio on Instagram. He's the tech and sound guy, nicknamed **"Flash"** because he solves every technical problem.
- **Andrea** is from **Sanremo**. Antonio introduced him, and he's part of the band's creative core.
- "We all work together; we're a family now."

## Alina (vocals, lyrics)
- Ukrainian. She has lived in **Rome since 2011**. She left Ukraine and wanted nothing more to do with it.
- When the war broke out in 2022 it still hurt her deeply, and that's when she wrote the lyrics of "Nebo".

## Nikolai (guitar, management)
- Russian, grew up in Paris. His family is from **Saint Petersburg**, but **Fissenko is a Ukrainian surname from Donbas**. "The irony: a Ukrainian and a Russian in the same band, and the Russian is half Ukrainian himself."
- His Russian family is split between patriotism towards Russia and simply not wanting a war.

## The song
- "Nebo" means "sky" in Ukrainian. Three sergeants in three armies refuse to fire. "We are an ocean against a drop of evil." Out Fri 23/10/2026, 145 BPM, 3:12.
- **The sergeants are invented, but the voices in the song are real:** voice notes sent to the band by friends living in those countries.

## Handle with care
- **Don't use "civil war" in any pitch.** Nikolai privately sees it as "almost a civil war" (his international-relations view). To Ukrainian readers and editors that phrase echoes Kremlin framing and would sink the pitch. Keep it out of emails; it's Nikolai's call in a live interview, where he has room to explain.
- Don't add details that aren't on this page: no city in Ukraine, no family specifics beyond what's here.
- Pitches stay universal ("every army"). See MANAGEMENT_BRIEF §0.
