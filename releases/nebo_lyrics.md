## NOTES ON LYRICS

**Language:** English + Ukrainian + Russian — the final refusal delivered in all three simultaneously. Do not translate the Ukrainian and Russian lines into English in any promo material — leave them untranslated. The non-English speaker not understanding is part of the point.

**Chorus variation:** First chorus ends "May we hold" (uncertain, a prayer). Second ends "We will hold" (resolved, a statement). Intentional — do not flatten to one version.

**Nebo (небо):** Ukrainian for "sky." The sky is the one territory that belongs to no army, no border, no flag. The title is never explained in the lyrics — it lives above the song.

**Title in four languages for artwork:**
- Ukrainian: НЕБО
- Russian: НЕБО  
- Arabic: سماء
- Hebrew: שָׁמַיִם

Separator for single line: НЕБО • سماء • שָׁמַיִם • НЕБО
